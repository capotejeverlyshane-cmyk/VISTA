The title is an essential component in every research proposal since it hints to readers
whether they will read your paper or not. It should present read ers with a clear and
concise description of the entire project. The following are some pointers of how to
construct a good title (Nair, 2014):
Contain as few words as possible: many journals limit tags to 12 words
Be easy to understand
Describe the contents of the paper accurately and specifically
Avoid abbreviations, formulas, and jargon
Not include any verbDOCUMENT

The scope of the study discusses the study's inclusions and coverage, which include, but
are not limited to, (1) the possible location of deployment, (2) potential users of the
system, and (3) an overview of the processes involved. The discussion of the scope should
be limited to one paragraph.
The limitations are exclusions from the study that were beyond the developers' control,
which include but are not limited to (1) processes involving or related to the system that is
excluded in the development, (2) technology limitation (i.e., data mining or machine
learning features  limitation, GIS functionality limitation, game design constraints), (3)
platform to be used (web-based, windows-based, online or offline), (4) type of data to be
used in the study , and (5) non-functional requirements. Non-functio nal requirements are
descriptions of the system's opera tion capabilities and limits that improve its functionality.
These can include things like speed, security, and dependability.  Scope and Limitation

Synthesis of key themes: In this section, the review will synthesize the existing
literature/ works by identifying key themes or patterns that emerge across multiple
sources. This may involve summ arizing common findings (i.e., the result of the
algorithm/s and the parameters used).
Identification of gaps: The review will also identify gaps in the current literature/work
and areas where there is a lack of research or conflicting findings. This helps justify the
current study's need and sets the stage for the research question . Research gap is
usually found under recommendation.
Conclusion: In the conclusion, sum marize the key findings you have taken from the
literature and emphasize their significance.
Note : Separate the discussion of the Related Literature and Related works. Related
literature refers to a critical analysis of the existing literature related to the study's
objectives. It is an overview of the relevant literature, identifies key themes, and identifies
gaps or areas where further research is needed. In contrast, the related works refer to
other systems similar in the proposed system's style, genre, or subject matter.

This section includes the description of the analysis phase of the systems development of
the project and must depict the following:
System Architecture
A system architecture explains the process of the system, users, and other system
components interacting using the application.   It is a conceptual framework that
describes the structure and behavior of the proposed system. Moreover, this
section describes the system components, including hardware, software, and data
components. It includes diagrams or other visual representations to help illustrate
the architecture.
Functional and non-functional requirements
This section should discuss the system's expected system functionalities (functional
requirements) and expected quality attributes (non-functional requirements).
Provide a brief discussion and numbered list form of the two requirements.
Use Case Diagram
A Use Case Diagram highlights the details of system users' (also known as actors)
and interactions with the proposed system.  
Context Flow Diagram (Level 0 Data Flow Diagram)
The Context Flow Diagram in the current system depicts the flow of business
actions (logical). It specifies the business events and the data each event requires
and produces. It also shows how the system interacts with external entities
(system, organiza tion groups, external data stores, etc.). However, the physical data
flow will be utilized if the theme falls under game development.
Level 1 Data Flow Diagram  
The first explosio n of the Level 0 DFD is Level 1. It shows the Level 1 DFD in greater
detail and depicts the system's basic modules and data flow. The system is broken
down into sub-pr ocesses in this diagram. Note that the entities and data coming in
and out from the entity in the Level 0 DFD should retain in Level 1 DFD.
Note : For logical diagrams, Level 0 and Level 1 DFD, use the Gane and Sarson method.  System Analysis

THIS CAPSTONE GUIDELINE IS INTENDED ONLY FOR THE STUDENTS USE WHO ARE OFFICIALLY ENROLLED IN THE IT 323
CAPSTONE PROJECT 1 COURSE (AY 2022 - 2023). NOT FOR DISTRIBUTION OUTSIDE ITS INTENDED USE.Title Defense
During the title defense, the students are expected to present their proposed Capstone
Project study's three (3) concept papers to the panelists. The following is the outline of the
presentation during the title defense:
Outline Description
Introduction The proponents must be able to introduce their names to the panelists.
Concept of the three (3)
Capstone Project studyThe concept of the Capstone Project study includes the following:
Project Title
Rationale and brief background of the problem statement, locale
and overview of the project. The presentation should be based from
the Problem Tree Analysis, Solution Tree Analysis and MCUA Matrix
Objectives of the Study
Scope and Limitation
Comparative Table with existing system and proposed system (if
possible)
Question and AnswerThe proponents are expected to answer the question thrown by the
panelists relevant to the proposed Capstone Project. The panelists will
ask the proponents on the overview of the study (including the current
process flow of the organization relative to the study), novelty or main
contribution of the proponents , objectives of the study and possible
methodologies.

PROJECT
Guidelines
THIS CAPSTONE GUIDELINE IS INTENDED ONLY FOR THE STUDENTS USE WHO ARE OFFICIALLY ENROLLED IN THE IT 323
CAPSTONE PROJECT 1 COURSE (AY 2022 - 2023). NOT FOR DISTRIBUTION OUTSIDE ITS INTENDED USE.Outline Defense
During the outline defense, the students are expected to present their approved Capstone
Project Title and System prototype  . The following is the outline of the presentation during
the outline defense:
Outline Description
Introduction The proponents must be able to introduce their names to the panelists.
Executive SummaryThe executive summary is a brief background of the capstone project.
This details the following:
Project Title
Rationale and brief background of the problem statement
Objectives of the Study
Comparative presentation of the Capstone project and existing
system
Brief discussion of the methodologies (Functional and non-
functional requirements, Conceptual framework, use case diagram,
CFD and Level 1 DFD, ERD/ JSON Schema Diagram, Technologies
used)
System prototype walkthrough
and Simulation presentationThe proponents will walkthrough the panelists to their system
prototype based on the functional requirements of the system.
Simulation of the model/ sensors will follow.
Question and AnswerThe proponents are expected to answer the question thrown by the
panelists relevant to the system prototype and model simulation. The
panelists will ask the proponents about the system's flow, the
algorithm used in the model (including parameters) or sensors used,
and other pertinent questions relative to the title.
