Data Privacy Policy

In compliance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), the Local Government Unit (LGU) is committed to protecting your personal information. By using the VISTA Chatbot, you agree to the following terms:

1. Information We Collect

We collect data necessary to provide and improve our services, including:

  • Chat logs (the questions you ask and the answers provided).
  • Your selected language preferences (English, Tagalog, Bisaya).
  • Basic profile information (Name, Email) if you choose to log in via Firebase Authentication.
  • Feedback ratings you submit.

2. How We Use Your Information

The data collected is used strictly for:

  • Responding accurately to your civic inquiries.
  • Improving the AI model through retraining (using unmatched queries).
  • Generating anonymous LGU analytics to understand citizen concerns.

3. Data Security

Your chat logs and feedback are securely stored in our encrypted databases. Only authorized LGU Administrators have access to this data for review and analytics purposes.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting the LGU Administrator.
